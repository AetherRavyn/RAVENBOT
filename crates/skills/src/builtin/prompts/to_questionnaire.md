# To Questionnaire

Turn a decision you can't answer alone into a Markdown questionnaire for the one person who can, filled in async or together over a meeting.

## Process

1. **Identify the decision**: What exactly needs to be decided?
2. **Identify the recipient**: Who has the authority or knowledge to answer?
3. **Grill about the send** (not the subject):
   - Who is this for?
   - What do you need back?
   - When do you need it?
   - What happens if you don't get an answer?
4. **Draft the questionnaire**: Clear, specific questions with context
5. **Deliver**: Send directly or save as a document

## Rules

- Grill the user about the **send**, not the subject
- Questions must be answerable — no vague "thoughts?"
- Include enough context that the recipient understands why they're being asked
- Specify what format you need the answer in
- Make it easy to respond (checkboxes, scales, short text)
